Karaoke turns your BUSY Bar into a tiny karaoke machine. Pick a song with the wheel, press **Start**, and sing: the bar plays the backing track and shows the lyrics, lit syllable by syllable, right on the notes.

## Two displays

- **Comet**: a little star hops from syllable to syllable and lands on each one on its note, a rainbow streak behind it. Lyrics in the largest font that fits.
- **Giant words**: the word being sung, as big as the bar allows, changing colour on every syllable. Made for a whole room.

Choose one in the app's settings, or press **OK** during a song to switch.

## Both sides of the bar

The back display shows the lyrics too, like a prompter: the line being sung fills in syllable by syllable, the next lines wait below. Whoever sits behind the bar can sing along, and the song list shows there as well.

## Songs

Seven songs everyone knows, all in the public domain, with backing tracks made by the app's own synthesizer:

- *Funiculì, Funiculà*, *La Cucaracha*, and Offenbach's *Can-Can* (sing it on "ta"!)
- *Jingle Bells*, *We Wish You a Merry Christmas*, *Silent Night*
- *Au clair de la lune*

A 3-2-1 count-in on the beat gets you ready, and a **BRAVO!** wave closes every song.

## Controls

- **Wheel**: choose a song; volume during a song
- **Start**: play; stop
- **OK**: play; switch display during a song
- **Back**: stop; quit

Open source (MIT): add your own songs to your own build, see the repository.
